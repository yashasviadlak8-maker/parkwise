3. Your scope: Users, Vehicles, Scale and Justification
3.1 Tables
Port these nine tables from the Oracle script, with the same CHECK and UNIQUE constraints:
	•	user_master: phone and email UNIQUE, user_type CHECK IN (REGISTERED, ATTENDANT, ADMIN).
	•	registered_user (KYC status, wallet balance of 0 or more, default payment method), admin_user, and parking_attendant with the extra column zone_id next to lot_id (its FK to parking_lot goes in 99_fk_C.sql). The subtype tables reference user_master and cascade on delete.
	•	vehicle: license_plate UNIQUE, vehicle_type CHECK IN (CAR, BIKE, TRUCK, EV), and UNIQUE (vehicle_id, user_id) because B's composite FK depends on it.
	•	vehicle_car, and the bike, truck and electric vehicle subtype tables, each with their own attributes and a cascading FK to vehicle.
Stubs for solo testing (revenue function only): zone_config(zone_id, zone_name), parking_slot(zone_id, slot_id, lot_id), booking(booking_id, zone_id, slot_id) and payment(payment_id, booking_id, amount, status, payment_timestamp) filled with about 10 hand-made rows, including a REFUNDED payment and a zone with no payments. Also a stub parking_lot(zone_id, lot_id) with ids 1-50 for the attendant FK.
3.2 Data
Write data/generate_c.py (Faker with a fixed seed, CSV output, load with COPY):
	•	1,500 users by the id ranges in the contract: Indian names, 10-digit phone numbers, unique emails, created_at spread over the last year; 1,400 registered users (wallet 0 to 2,000, mixed KYC status), 60 attendants (lots 1-50 assigned in rotation, with zone_id from the lot rule), 40 admins.
	•	2,000 vehicles following the owner and type ranges in the contract, plates like TN 22 AB 1234, about 80% verified, each with exactly one matching subtype row.
Write tests/validate_c.sql: every user has exactly one matching subtype row (this proves the disjoint and total specialization), every vehicle has exactly one subtype row, and no duplicate phone, email or plate.
3.3 Logic (03_logic.sql or core/users_crdb.py)
	•	register_vehicle(user_id, plate, type, attr1, attr2, attr3): checks the user exists and is REGISTERED, checks the plate format ^[A-Z]{2} [0-9]{2} [A-Z]{1,3} [0-9]{4}$ after upper-casing, inserts the vehicle and its subtype row in one transaction (own conn.transaction() with a retry loop, as in the contract), and returns the new id.
	•	topup_wallet(user_id, amount): amount must be positive and at most 10,000; the user must be REGISTERED with KYC VERIFIED; adds to the balance and returns it.
	•	zone_monthly_revenue(zone, month, year): sum of SUCCESS payments through booking, slot, lot and zone, filtered by payment month and year, minus REFUNDED amounts in that month; returns 0 (not NULL) when empty.
